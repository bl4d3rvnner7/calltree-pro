## Quick navigation

- **Start at the entry point:** click the ★ `vq.execute` in the sidebar of `html.html` (it's auto-selected on load).
- **Drill into a callee:** click it in any tree → use **◄ back** in the navbar to return.
- **Severity overview:** the right panel of `html.html` shows the shortest path to every dangerous sink reachable from your selection.
- **Source lines:** unresolved callees show the exact line in the source where they're invoked (e.g. `dns.resolve4 L1234`). Hover the function header for full file:line:column.
- **Render the SVG manually:** `dot -Tsvg graph.dot -o graph.svg` or `mmdc -i graph.mmd -o graph.svg`.
